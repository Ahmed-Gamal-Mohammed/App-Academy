🔁 Quick Express Middleware Rules Recap

    Normal middleware (with 3 arguments): app.use((req, res, next) => {}) or app.get(...)

        Calls next() → goes to the next normal middleware.

        Calls next(error) or throw error → goes to the next error-handling middleware ((err, req, res, next)).

    Error-handling middleware (4 arguments): app.use((err, req, res, next) => {})

        Only runs after an error is passed via next(err) or a thrown error.

        next() → goes to the next error-handling middleware.

    Once a response is sent (res.send()), nothing else happens — middleware after that is ignored.
