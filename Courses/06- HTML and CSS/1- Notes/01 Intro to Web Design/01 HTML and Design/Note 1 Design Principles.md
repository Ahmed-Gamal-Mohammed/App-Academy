## Dieter Rams's 10 principles for good design:
Good design...

1. is innovative (مبتكر )
2. makes a product useful (يجعل المنتج مفيد)
3. is aesthetic (جمالي)
4. makes a product understandable (يجعل المنتج مفهوم)
5. is unobtrusive (غير مزعج)
6. is honest (صادق)
7. is long-lasting (طويل الامد\)
8. is thorough down to the last detail (شامل لاخر التفاصيل)
9. is environmentally friendly (صديق للبيئه)
10. is minimal (هو الحد الادني)
## Modern web design principles
Although this is not a design course, here are a few universal design principles to keep in mind while building websites. Remember Dieter Rams's principles: your website should be simple, accessible and intuitive.
### White Space
The most important design principle for intuitive design is [white space](https://www.seguetech.com/whitespace-web-design/). Put simply, leaving empty space surrounding the text, images and interactive elements in your website significantly increases the legibility and usability.

This is especially important when it comes to websites on mobile devices. It's easy to mis-tap buttons that are too close, and difficult to read text that is crammed too close together. Keep your interfaces clean, simple and intuitive with white space.
### Colors
There are two major considerations when choosing colors for your website: _aesthetics_ and _accessibility_.

**Aesthetics**: Your colors should complement each other, not clash. Picking complementary colors is an art but fortunately for those without color theory backgrounds, there are tools to help you pick colors that look great. The [Adobe color wheel](https://color.adobe.com/create/color-wheel) can help you customize a set of matching colors while [Google's material design docs](https://material.io/design/color/the-color-system.html) will guide you toward applying those colors appropriately in your website.

**Accessibility**: Your colors should be easily distinguishable, even for the color-blind. Color-blindness affects 300 million people world-wide. To achieve this, your colors should be distinct in hue, saturation and value. To test how an image looks from a color-blind perspective, try the [color-blindness simulator](https://www.color-blindness.com/coblis-color-blindness-simulator/)
### Typography

The most important consideration for typography (besides proper use of white space) is choosing readable fonts. Fonts can be divided into two major categories: serif and sans-serif.

![Times New Roman](https://appacademy-open-assets.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/Modular-Curriculum/content/css/Times_New_Roman-sample.svg)

**Serif Fonts**: Serifs are the small strokes on the ends of letters in fonts like _Times New Roman_. The purpose of serifs dates back to old printing presses and were intended to improve readability of text blocks printed with low-quality methods, like newspapers and early books. These days, serifs are still used for newsprint and to improve long-form readability on mobile or low-resolution devices. Modern variants include Microsoft's [Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_\(typeface\)), Apple's [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_\(2019_typeface\)) and Google's [Roboto Slab](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto+Slab) fonts.

![Helvetica](https://appacademy-open-assets.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/Modular-Curriculum/content/css/HelveticaSpecimenCH.svg)

**Sans-Serif Fonts**: Sans-serif fonts have no serifs. These are minimal and clean: ideal for single words or short-form phrases like headlines, icons, navigation. These tend toward larger text size and shorter strings of words, making serifs unnecessary. With modern high-resolution screens, serifs are falling out of style even for long-form text. _Helvetica_ is one of the most famous sans-serif fonts, along with modern variants like Microsoft's [Verdana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verdana), Apple's [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_\(sans-serif_typeface\)) and Google's [Roboto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roboto)

### Conventions

Trends in web design come and go and as a web developer, you should be aware of common conventions. For example, three stacked horizontal lines represents a [menu icon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburger_button) and should only be used for that purpose.

The best way to stay on top of shifting trends is to visit many different websites with a conscious and curious eye toward the iconography and design principles. Websites are updated frequently to keep up with trends in web design and as a front end developer, staying up-to-date will ensure you always have a job.
