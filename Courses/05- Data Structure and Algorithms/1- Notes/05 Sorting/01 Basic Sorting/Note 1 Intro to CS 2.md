## Problem solving
Every problem can be solved with Polya's problem solving framework. The projects over the coming days will take you on a deep dive of steps 2 and 3.
1. Understand the problem
2. **Come up with a plan**
3. **Execute the plan**
4. Iterate and improve your solution

**how do you improve at coming up with plans?**
One way is to study classic algorithms. You will learn many ways to solve a simple problem like sorting array values in numerical order.
## Asking questions
Because these are classic algorithms, you will find solutions readily available online. The point is not to memorize or copy/paste them, it is to _understand_ and _implement_ them.

These algorithms are clever, elegant and probably unlike any code you have ever seen or written before. You will be expected to implement these algorithms in very specific ways and _understanding_ these constraints may be difficult at times.

As always, it's important to ask questions for clarity. The traits of a good question are:

1. It's specific
2. It's clear and concise
3. It shows that you've put work into it
